Individual analytics coursework · FIT3152 · 2026

AI & Employment · Text Analytics

Explore how academic papers, blogs and news articles discuss AI and employment.

My role / scope
Collected and labelled the corpus, developed the original R analysis and interpreted clustering, sentiment and network representations.
Evidence to inspect
Recomputed clustering visual and the distinction between sample patterns and statistical conclusions.
Context
Exploratory 20-document corpus; no causal or labour-market prediction claim.

A small corpus, several representations

The sample contains seven academic documents, six blog posts and seven news articles. The workflow covers text cleaning, stemming, a retained document-term matrix, cosine-distance clustering and sentiment/network analysis.

Interpret the result carefully

Clusters mix publishing genres; recomputed purity is 0.55. Purity describes cluster composition and is not predictive accuracy. The reported sentiment comparison gives p = 0.0909, which does not establish a genre difference at the 5% threshold.

Raw sentiment totals can be affected by document length and vocabulary. My next step would be length-normalised scoring and a review of sample overlap, rather than turning a descriptive chart into a broad claim about public opinion.
