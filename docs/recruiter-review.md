# Recruiter-lens review · 19 November 2026

This is a simulated recruitment review, not an external HR endorsement.

| Screening question | Previous weakness | Implemented change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which role does this candidate fit? | Broad business/technology headline. | Early-career Business / Systems Analyst positioning and a primary analyst resume. |
| What has the candidate done? | Five older cases; new analytical and UX evidence absent. | Four featured cases and six supporting cases, each with a purpose. |

## Remaining evidence gaps

- RespiraCare individual feature ownership needs contribution records before stronger resume claims.
- Coursework outputs demonstrate analytical method; no real-client savings or forecast accuracy are implied.
- Employment dates and contact details are preserved from the existing portfolio rather than newly verified with employers.
- Actual client screenshots, original handover documents and detailed assessment files remain restricted.

The practical hiring assessment is that BA/System Analyst fit is now easier to assess. It is still an early-career portfolio: evidence quality and clarity improve screening, but do not establish professional seniority or guarantee an interview.
